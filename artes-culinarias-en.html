Program Description

Culinary Arts is currently one of the most in-demand career fields. The food industry is growing worldwide and the opportunities are many and varied. The course aims to train students and develop the skills they need to work with confidence and professionalism. The Culinary Arts curriculum covers the basics of the history of gastronomy and employee hygiene, foodborne illnesses, planning and effective use of time, preparing a variety of menus, reading recipes, choosing and caring for kitchen utensils, purchasing and storage, applying cooking principles, and much more.

Admission Requirements

  • Overall GPA of 2.00 or higher
  • Admission application
  • Original 7th grade transcript and a recent Progress Report
  • Personal knowledge and basic skills test
  • Interview with the workshop teacher (if needed)

Why choose Culinary Arts?

Gastronomy is an art that combines creativity, technique and passion. Our program prepares you to be a well-rounded professional, able to create memorable culinary experiences and run successful food businesses.
