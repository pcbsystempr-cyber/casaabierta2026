Program Description

The Nursing program trains professionals to provide comprehensive patient care, combining scientific knowledge with hands-on health care skills. You will develop the skills to work in a variety of health care settings with compassion and professional excellence.

Admission Requirements

  • GPA: 3.00 or higher
  • Interest in learning about nursing
  • Admission interview

Why choose Nursing?

Nursing is a noble profession that saves lives and improves people's quality of life. Our program combines a solid academic foundation with hands-on experience to prepare you for a rewarding career in health care.
