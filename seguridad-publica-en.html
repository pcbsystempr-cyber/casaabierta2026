Everything you need to know about Public Safety: what you will learn, requirements and opportunities.

What is Public Safety?

Public safety is the protection the government gives people and their rights so they can live in peace and free from danger. It is also the job we all share of looking after our community.

Workshop Purpose

To train students with the knowledge, skills and abilities in public safety so they can eventually join the country's public service workforce.
