Get to know the workshop

Everything you need to know about Cosmetology: what you will learn, requirements and opportunities.

Program Description

The course covers the teaching and learning of all the theoretical and practical knowledge, along with the skills and values of a beauty specialist, in accordance with the principles and concepts set by the standards and competencies that form the foundation of the profession. For this reason, the course aims for students to fully master both academic and occupational skills.

It meets the requirements of the Board of Examiners of Beauty Specialists so students can take the licensing exam and become licensed in the profession. At the end of the course, students earn a certificate of hours worked in addition to their high school diploma.

Course Length

The course is offered during 10th, 11th and 12th grade, during which students complete 1,000 hours. The course includes Special Education students.

Admission Requirements

  • GPA: 2.00 or higher and completion of 8th grade.
  • Interest in learning about cosmetology.
  • Admission interview.
