Message from the Principal

Israel López Rivera

School Principal

Dear students and families: Welcome to the Open House of Pablo Colón Berdecia Vocational High School. Today you are not just visiting a school: you are discovering the place where your dreams begin to take shape. Here we believe in the talent of every young person. In our workshops hands learn, minds transform and hearts discover their purpose. Everyone who walks through our gates finds committed teachers, real opportunities and a family that believes in their potential. It doesn't matter where you come from; what matters is where you want to go. Dare to dream big, to work with passion and to never give up. The future isn't something you wait for: you build it, and here we give you the tools to build it. We welcome you with open arms! La vieja marcando la diferencia. (The old school, making the difference.)

Israel López Rivera
