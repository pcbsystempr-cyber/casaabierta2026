Get to know the workshop

Everything you need to know about Marketing: what you will learn, requirements and opportunities.

Program Description

Mission: To prepare students with the skills they need to excel in sales, customer service and marketing-related fields, fostering leadership, initiative, professional ethics and responsibility.

Vision: To be a leading program in preparing highly competent technical students who can join the workforce or continue their studies in business fields with a solid foundation in marketing, sales and service.

To strengthen students' spirit of service and civic awareness so they become more caring and supportive of others.

Admission Requirements

  • Minimum overall GPA of 2.00
  • Good conduct and willingness to work as a team
  • Interest in sales, customer service or business
  • Join the program starting in ninth grade

Career Opportunities

Students who complete this specialty can work in areas such as:

  • Sales representative
  • Customer service associate
  • Cashier
  • Promoter or merchandiser
  • Office assistant or marketing assistant
  • Jobs in department stores, supermarkets and service companies
  • Entry-level positions in advertising, inventory and online sales

Why choose Marketing?

  • It is a dynamic, hands-on workshop focused on real experiences
  • It builds the essential skills every employer looks for
  • It strengthens communication, self-confidence and leadership
  • It prepares you for competitions and certifications through DECA
  • You can take an active part in the Youth Cooperative
  • It expands your job and college opportunities
  • It shapes creative, proactive students who are ready for the workplace
